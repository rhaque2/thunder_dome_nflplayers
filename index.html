Trade Target

Pick your team, then type a player you want from another team (add a second to target a pair). You’ll see the packages from your roster most likely to get a deal done, ranked by how much the other team’s lineup improves.

Trade Block

Type a player you want to shop (add a second or third to shop a package). You’ll get fair offers from every other team: 1-for-1, 1-for-2, 2-for-1, 2-for-2, 2-for-3 or 3-for-3. Offers are ranked by how much both lineups improve.

Trade values come from the Week 11 PPR value chart, adjusted for half PPR (RB +4%, WR −6%, TE −10%); unlisted players get a low fallback and K/D/ST are never traded. Numbers in parentheses are package values (best player plus half the second). A deal needs both sides within 15% in value, both starting lineups up by at least 0.5 projected pts/wk (ESPN Week 11 projections, * = bye or injury estimate), and elite RB/WR (68+) only move for an RB/WR of similar value, never for a lower-value QB or TE. “Near-miss” trades are fair on value but cost one side a little lineup now. Lineup: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), D/ST, K.

Loyalty lines

  • Shosh and Alvin Kamara: drafted in 6 of Shosh’s 7 drafts, from a first-rounder (2020–22) to round 3 (2024–25) to his final pick of round 16 in 2026.
  • MoCho and Michael Thomas: drafted in 5 different drafts, first as a 2020 first-rounder and last as a round-16 flier in 2024.
  • Habeeb and Travis Kelce: round 2 (2022), round 4 (2024), round 8 (2025), round 12 (2026).
  • Akash and Josh Jacobs: drafted in 2021–22 and again in 2025–26, taking 2023–24 off in between.
  • Julio and Stefon Diggs: drafted 4 times (2020, 2021, 2025, 2026), skipping the middle years.
  • Shosh and the Bills D/ST: five straight drafts, 2021–25.

Draft-DNA lines

  • Umer: never a QB or TE in the first 4 rounds in 8 drafts. In 2023 he named his team “QB1”, took Justin Fields in round 5, finished last, and spent 24 hours in Waffle House.
  • Shakib: never a QB before round 6, and the most WR-heavy early drafter.
  • Habeeb: RB in round 1 in 6 of 8 drafts. His two WR first-rounders were Tyreek Hill (2021) and CeeDee Lamb (2025, the title year).
  • Ruhul: the only owner to regularly pay for a round-3 QB (2019, 2020, 2023). Two of those three seasons ended with a trophy.
  • Tahmeed: a QB in round 3 or 4 in every season he’s played.
  • Ruhul on defense: takes his D/ST earliest, about round 12.
  • Tanvir: Ashton Jeanty at 9th overall started his 2025 last-place finish.

Rivalry tie-ins

  • Julio and Shosh’s first trade: Jordan Mason and Brandon Aubrey were both Julio’s 2026 draft picks (round 10 and round 13).
  • Habeeb vs. Tanvir: Habeeb is the most RB-heavy first-rounder, while Tanvir’s 2024 title came off a round-1 WR, Garrett Wilson at 9th overall.
