Trade Meter

Pick two owners and add every player each side gives, any number. You’ll get each owner’s odds of saying yes, the lineup effect, and the smallest tweaks (add, drop or swap a player) that would make the partner more likely to accept without wrecking your side. Tap a player chip to remove it.

Trade Target

Pick your team, then type a player you want from another team (add a second to target a pair). You’ll see the packages from your roster most likely to get a deal done, ranked by how much the other team’s lineup improves.

Trade Block

Type a player you want to shop (add a second or third to shop a package). You’ll get fair offers from every other team: 1-for-1, 1-for-2, 2-for-1, 2-for-2, 2-for-3 or 3-for-3. Offers are ranked by how much both lineups improve.

Trade values come from the Week 12 PPR value chart, adjusted for half PPR (RB +4%, WR −6%, TE −10%); unlisted players get a low fallback and K/D/ST are never traded. Numbers in parentheses are package values (best player plus half the second). A deal needs both sides within 15% in value, both starting lineups up by at least 0.5 projected pts/wk (ESPN Week 11 projections, * = bye or injury estimate), and elite RB/WR (68+) only move for an RB/WR of similar value, never for a lower-value QB or TE. “Near-miss” trades are fair on value but cost one side a little lineup now. Lineup: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), D/ST, K.

3-Team Trade Finder

Three owners each send one or two players around a circle (A→B→C→A). Strict mode needs every leg fair on value (within 15%, with the elite RB/WR rule) and all three lineups up at least 0.3 pts/wk. Near-miss mode widens fairness to 25% and lets one team lose up to 0.8 pts/wk or gain less than 0.3, as long as the circle adds at least 1 pt/wk overall. Use the filter to see circles that include your team.

Draft Retrospective

How each pick looks today. Every skill-position pick is compared with the typical current trade value of a pick from that round in the 2026 class; the gap is the pick’s surplus (Steal +20 or more, Hit +6, Fair, Miss −6, Bust −20 or worse). A player who is no longer rostered counts as value 0. Owner grades and the best/bust lists compare each pick with the average pick from the same draft year, so owners aren’t punished for drafting in older, mostly emptied-out classes. This is a snapshot of current value, not season-long production, so older classes naturally show more busts. K and D/ST are skipped, and letter grades compare owners against each other.
